About Us

Sunrise Public School was established in 2005 with a mission to provide holistic education with modern values. Our dedicated faculty and well-equipped infrastructure ensure an engaging and safe learning environment.

We focus on academic excellence, extracurricular growth, and character development to help every student reach their full potential.
